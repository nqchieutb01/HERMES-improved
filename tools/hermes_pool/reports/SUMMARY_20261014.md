Full tables with paired 95% bootstrap intervals (* = excludes 0): `report_all_mss.md` (minute:second times read as
seconds; used below) and `report_all.md` (official S-EMBER parser). They differ only where the model writes
`Time: [1:45, 2:07]` on videos over a minute (QVHighlights, ActivityNet); method rankings are the same in both.
Regenerate: `cd /home/chieu.nguyen/HERMES && python3 /home/chieu.nguyen/hermes_pool/reports/report_all.py [--mss]`.

Method names: **Cover** = question-attention top-k inside each frame with equal per-frame quotas; **Retrieve** =
SigLIP 2 retrieval frames (half uniform); **PCD** = prior-contrastive decoding; **reserve** = answer reserve (with 64
tokens left and no `Answer:` line, write it); **final** = Retrieve + Cover + PCD + reserve.

## 3. The two fixes from phase 31

- **Answer reserve** (PCD answers lost their Time line at the 384-token cap): +1.8 to +6.1 mIoU*, significant on every
  dataset and method tested; unparsed answers go to 0. On S-EMBER it also adds +1.1 Acc.* for Random + PCD.
- **Coverage gate** (retrieval only when uniform frames are >= 1 s apart): no effect on S-EMBER (+0.0); on Charades it
  restores uniform frames (Retrieve + Cover 15.4 -> 16.4) but the gated final (23.3) is still 2.9* below Random + PCD +
  reserve (26.2). See limitations.

Question-aware selection gets *better* as the budget shrinks (Retrieve + Cover 31.0 -> 34.6) while random and SemVID
get worse: at 5% Retrieve + Cover is +13.7* over SemVID and +10.7* over random. The final method is flat from 10% to
5%; against SemVID + PCD + reserve it leads by +7.4* (5%), +4.5* (10%) and +1.5 (20%, n.s.); at 20% its gap to
Random + PCD + reserve also disappears (+0.3). The method's advantage is largest at tight budgets. QVHighlights: final 48.8 (10%) / 49.4 (20%).

## 5. Ablations

- **Per-layer vs shared token set** (input-level pruners share one set across layers; ours selects per layer):
  sharing does not change random (+0.7, n.s.) and costs the final method -1.5* mIoU. So random's advantage over the
  input-level pruners is not per-layer diversity, and per-layer selection is a small plus for Cover.
- **Random seeds**: random 10% mIoU 26.0 ± 0.6 (3 seeds), so gaps of 2+ points are beyond seed noise.
- Unequal per-frame quotas (phase 27, judged): no gain over equal quotas (pre-registered primary test -0.2 [-1.2, +0.8]).

## 5b. Coverage vs per-frame evidence at a fixed token budget (phase 36, added 10:50; official parser)

F frames at keep ratio r * 64 / F keep exactly the same tokens as 64 frames at r (checked: 2,843 = 2,843 on Charades),
so only the split between frames and tokens per frame changes.

| Same token budget | 64 frames | 32 frames | 16 frames |
|---|---|---|---|
| QVHighlights, ~260 tokens: final | 9.0 | **41.9** | 40.4 |
| QVHighlights: Random + PCD + reserve | — | 31.3 | 33.3 |
| QVHighlights: SemVID + PCD + reserve | — | 34.7 | 30.6 |
| ActivityNet, ~120-300 tokens: final | 11.4 | **33.7** | 32.0 |
| ActivityNet: Random + PCD + reserve | 19.3 | 29.0 | 30.5 |
| ActivityNet: SemVID + PCD + reserve | 26.3 | 28.3 | 30.0 |
| Charades, 64 frames at 10%: final (Cover + PCD + reserve, uniform frames) | 23.3 | 23.6 | — |
| Charades: Random + PCD + reserve | 26.1 | 24.6 | — |
| Charades: SemVID + PCD + reserve | 21.7 | 20.8 | — |

- The collapse below ~300 tokens is a coverage/evidence trade-off, not a failure of question-aware selection: with 32
  frames (~15 tokens per frame pair) the final method is the best method at that budget — QVH +10.6* over Random +
  PCD + reserve and +7.2* over SemVID + PCD + reserve; ActivityNet +4.7* and +5.4*. 32 frames is better than 16 for
  the final method on both.
- Working range observed: >= ~15 visual tokens per frame pair (S-EMBER 64 frames at 5% = ~33; collapse at ~8).
- Charades (short videos) at 32 frames: final 23.6 vs Random + PCD + reserve 24.6 (-0.9, n.s.; at 64 frames -2.9*),
  vs SemVID + PCD + reserve +2.9*, vs unpruned (32 frames) +8.4*.

## 6. Limitations to state

1. **Minimum tokens per frame** (resolved by using fewer frames at tight budgets, section 5b). Equal per-frame quotas collapse when the whole memory keeps fewer than ~300 tokens
   (~8 per frame pair): QVHighlights at 5% (260 tokens; final 9.7 mIoU) and ActivityNet at 10% (120-300 tokens; final
   11.6, below random 17.8 and SemVID + PCD 26.8). It works from ~500 tokens up (S-EMBER 5% = 1,070, QVH 10% = 520,
   ANet 30% = 400-900). The threshold is absolute, not a percentage: the QVHighlights and ActivityNet copies used here
   are low-resolution re-encodes (153 and ~84 tokens per frame pair vs 660 on S-EMBER).
2. **Short videos (Charades-STA, 30 s).** Every question-aware selector is at or below random; at 64 frames the final
   method is 2.9* below Random + PCD + reserve, at 32 frames 0.9 below (n.s.). With 64 frames over 30 s, spreading tokens beats concentrating them.
3. ActivityNet subset: videos from the first tar part of friedrichor/ActivityNet_Captions (224 px, 3 fps), one
   val_1 sentence per video; QVHighlights: single-window val queries only (1,020 of 1,550). Both are 500-query subsets.
