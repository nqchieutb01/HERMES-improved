No single operator is new; their combination for grounding is · input-level pruners keep one set for every layer

This slide positions the work honestly. Per-frame budgets exist (VidCom2, LGTTP), question attention exists (PruneVid, SnapKV), and contrastive decoding exists (VCD, M3ID). None of them targets grounding's two failures together: lost timestamps and the temporal prior. Our differences are the equal floor tied to timestamp survival, the real question's exact attention per layer, and contrast against no video restricted to time values with adaptive strength.
