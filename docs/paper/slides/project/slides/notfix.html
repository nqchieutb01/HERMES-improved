Three tempting fixes fail. Telling the model in the prompt changes nothing. Giving it the perfect tokens raises mIoU only 2 points and the leak stays. Even filling the whole memory with evidence leaves a 45% leak. PCD, with no gold information at all, beats the oracle selector by 4.7 mIoU. That is the motivation for fixing decoding, not just selection.
