Each of the three choices was tested against alternatives. The equal quota is not beaten by any relevance-weighted variant (the best is +0.5, not significant), so we keep the simplest one. The real question matters far more than the quota shape: generic probe questions give 18.3. Per-layer selection helps question-driven scores (sharing costs 1.5 for CoCo) and does nothing for random selection.
