Random and SemVID degrade as tokens are removed; question-driven selection improves (31.0 → 34.6): pruning removes distractors.

CoCo’s lead over SemVID + Contrast: +7.4* at 5%, +4.5* at 10%, +1.5 (n.s.) at 20%.

* = 95% paired bootstrap excludes 0 · bold blue = best, underline = second best per column

A useful efficiency point: CoCo is as good at 5% of the tokens as at 10%, and its advantage is largest at the tightest budget. Removing tokens the question does not attend to also removes distractors.
