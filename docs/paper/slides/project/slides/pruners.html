All four published pruners fall below random on S-EMBER, QVHighlights and Charades. They empty frames, and a frame without tokens loses its timestamp.

Honest limits: Coverage ties random on 30 s Charades videos, and at ActivityNet 10% (only 2–4 tokens per frame) it collapses; see Findings.

Part 2 starts with the other half of the problem. PCD removes the prior, but the memory decides how much evidence there is. Pruners built for QA keep salient or unique tokens and empty whole frames; for grounding, that removes timestamps and the timeline gets holes. Random selection keeps a few tokens in every frame, which is why it beats all four. Our Coverage stage is designed for this.
