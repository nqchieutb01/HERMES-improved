Remove the evidence, and the prior takes over

Causal test: drop the frames inside the gold interval (“evidence”), or as many frames elsewhere (“control”). Every other frame keeps its true timestamp.

Without evidence, answers do not spread out: they move to the start of the video (+14 points of leak).

PCD halves the fallback but cannot invent evidence: mIoU stays near 10 without it.

Leak: starts in the first 5% although the evidence starts later (397 questions) · bold blue = best, underline = second best per column

This is the causal link. Removing the evidence frames does not make the model answer randomly; it makes it answer at the start of the video, exactly the blind habit. Removing the same number of other frames does not. PCD, introduced next, halves this fallback in both cases.
