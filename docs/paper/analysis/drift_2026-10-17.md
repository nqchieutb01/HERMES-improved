# Meaning-drift check: prose rewrite vs 97517c4 (docs/paper/latex/sec/*.tex)

I read every changed passage in all 10 files (git diff --word-diff 97517c4). Almost all edits are style only:
sentence splitting, active voice, "against" changed to "compared with", headers turned into neutral labels.
All hedges are still in place: "on these queries", "n.s.", "in the timeline format", "partial", "earlier rule",
"so far", "on the 584 queries run so far", "in the settings we tested", "those runs had no answer reserve",
"cross-cluster pairs", "interval only just excludes 0". No number moved to a different quantity, model or
comparison.

I found no high-severity drift. There are four low-severity items. Each one adds or strengthens a
causal or firm claim slightly.

## 1. 6b_transfer.tex:110-112 -- conclusion moved away from its evidence (low-medium)
- Old: "...above the 2 fps low-resolution input capped at 4,500 tokens (+3.2*, +2.0*). With the protocol input
  instead of BOLT frames, a budget of 8.2% is filled by about 1.2k timestamp tokens, no visual token survives, and
  both selectors collapse (4.6 and 4.2 mIoU): at small budgets, fewer frames are needed (\cref{sec:frames})."
- New: "...($+3.2^*$ and $+2.0^*$). Small budgets need fewer frames (\cref{sec:frames}). With the protocol input
  instead of BOLT frames, about 1.2k timestamp tokens use up a budget of 8.2%, so no visual token survives and both
  selectors collapse (4.6 and 4.2 mIoU)."
- Why: in the old text, "fewer frames are needed" was the lesson drawn from the protocol-input collapse. In the new
  text it comes right after the 1.2k-vs-4.5k BOLT comparison. A reader can take it as the explanation for the
  BOLT+Coverage loss at 1.2k tokens (-2.5*, -2.1*), which the paper does not claim.
- Fix: move the sentence back after the collapse. For example: "...both selectors collapse (4.6 and 4.2 mIoU).
  At small budgets, fewer frames are needed (\cref{sec:frames})."

## 2. 6_results.tex:631-632 -- borderline accuracy difference stated as a firm gain (low)
- Old: "Over the oracle selector with PCD, \method is level on grounding ($+0.5$ mIoU, $+2.7$ GQ@0.5, n.s.;
  accuracy $+4.8$, interval lower bound 0.0)."
- New: "...level on grounding, with $+0.5$ mIoU and $+2.7$ GQ@0.5 (n.s.). Its accuracy is $+4.8$ higher, with an
  interval lower bound of 0.0."
- Why: the old text gave the +4.8 as a bare number without an asterisk, beside its lower bound of 0.0. "Its
  accuracy is +4.8 higher" reads as a firm result even though the interval reaches 0.
- Fix: "The accuracy difference is $+4.8$, but its interval reaches down to 0.0."

## 3. 6_results.tex:645 -- new causal link "As a result" (low)
- Old: "...for 41% of location questions (chance 13.5%). BOLT frames put 40% of the memory inside the evidence,
  against 36% for uniform frames."
- New: "...(chance 13.5\%). As a result, BOLT frames put 40\% of the memory inside the evidence, ..."
- Why: the old text listed the retrieval statistics (AUROC, top-1 hit rate) and BOLT's evidence share side by
  side. The new text says the first causes the second. That is plausible, but it is a new causal statement. The
  top-1 hit rate also does not set the share, since half of BOLT's frames are uniform.
- Fix: delete "As a result," so the sentence reads "BOLT frames put 40\% of the memory inside the evidence, ...".

## 4. 4_method.tex:53-55 -- "does not explain" became "cannot explain" (low)
- Old: "The benefit of per-layer selection is therefore specific to question-driven scores, and it does not explain
  why random selection beats input-level pruners."
- New: "Per-layer selection therefore helps question-driven scores specifically, and it cannot explain why random
  selection beats input-level pruners."
- Why: "cannot" turns an empirical finding (sharing leaves random selection unchanged, +0.7 n.s.) into a logical
  impossibility. It is slightly stronger than the evidence supports.
- Fix: "...and it does not explain why random selection beats input-level pruners."
