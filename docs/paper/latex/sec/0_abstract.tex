\begin{abstract}
Video LLMs prune visual tokens, and published pruners lose little
multiple-choice accuracy at 10\% of the tokens. Temporal grounding fares worse. A timestamp survives only if a visual
token of its frame survives, and QA pruners empty frames and
fall below random selection. In answers that list observations before the interval, a video-free prior fills the
gaps. We propose \method, a training-free method that selects frames by the question (BOLT), keeps the most
question-attended tokens per layer under an equal per-frame quota (Coverage), and contrasts time values with the
model's no-video prediction (PCD). With a one-line prompt, Coverage at 31\% of the tokens stays within 0.3 mIoU of the
uncompressed model on Charades-STA and QVHighlights. On reasoning-style grounded QA (S-EMBER) at 10\% of the tokens,
\method beats the uncompressed model by \key{$+11.2$} and \key{$+10.7$} mIoU (two models) with a timeline prompt, and
by \key{$+5.5$} with the official prompt. Gains are small on short, densely sampled videos, and PCD helps only where
the answer format lets the prior choose the times.
\end{abstract}
