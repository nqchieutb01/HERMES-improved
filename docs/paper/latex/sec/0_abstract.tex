\begin{abstract}
Video LLMs prune most of their visual tokens with little loss in multiple-choice accuracy, but temporal grounding
suffers. A timestamp survives only with a visual token of its frame, so QA pruners that empty frames fall below random
selection, and in reasoning-style answers a video-free prior fills the gaps. We propose \method, a training-free method
that selects frames by the question (BOLT), keeps the most question-attended tokens in every frame (Coverage), and
contrasts each written time with the model's no-video prediction (PCD). With 31\% of the tokens, Coverage alone matches
the uncompressed model on QVHighlights and Charades-STA. On grounded QA (S-EMBER) with 10\% of the tokens, \method
improves mIoU over the uncompressed model by up to \key{$+11.2$}. Gains are small on short videos.
\end{abstract}
