\section{Parsing times written as minutes:seconds}

numbers with the official parser are: QVHighlights, \method 44.6\,/\,50.2 (\vs 48.8\,/\,54.8); ActivityNet at 30\%,

\section{End-snapping on S-EMBER}
\label{sec:suppl-snap}

Setting every predicted end to the question time $q$ raises every S-EMBER method to 42--46 mIoU, more than the 39.3
of \method, because 67\% of the gold intervals end within the last fifth of the window. On closed intervals,
end-snapping \emph{hurts} every method (to 22--24 mIoU) while \method keeps 39.7; on QVHighlights it lowers \method
from 48.8 to 40.5. A decoder that decides whether an interval is still open at question time is a natural
extension. \TODO{open-interval decoding.}
