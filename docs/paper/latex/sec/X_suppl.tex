\section{Parsing times written as minutes:seconds}
\label{sec:suppl-parse}

On videos longer than a minute, the model sometimes writes times as minutes:seconds, \eg
\texttt{Time: [1:45, 2:07]}. The official S-EMBER parser scores such times as missing, so we convert \texttt{h:mm:ss}
and \texttt{m:ss} to seconds, for every method alike. With the official parser the method rankings do not change. The
main numbers become 44.6\,/\,50.2 for \method on QVHighlights (\vs 48.8\,/\,54.8) and 33.3\,/\,31.0 on ActivityNet

\section{End-snapping on S-EMBER}
\label{sec:suppl-snap}

Setting every predicted end to the question time $q$ raises every S-EMBER method to 42--46 mIoU, above the 39.3 of
\method. This works because 67\% of the gold intervals end within the last fifth of the window. On closed intervals,
end-snapping hurts every method and brings it down to 22--24 mIoU, while \method keeps 39.7. On QVHighlights it
lowers \method from 48.8 to 40.5. A natural extension is a decoder that decides whether an interval is still open
at question time. \TODO{open-interval decoding.}
