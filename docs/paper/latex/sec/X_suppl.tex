\section{Parsing times written as minutes:seconds}
\label{sec:suppl-parse}

On videos longer than a minute, the model sometimes writes times as minutes:seconds, \eg
\texttt{Time: [1:45, 2:07]}. The official S-EMBER parser scores such times as missing, so we convert \texttt{h:mm:ss}
and \texttt{m:ss} to seconds for every method. The official parser does not change the method rankings. It gives
\method in \cref{tab:ladder} (timeline prompt) 44.6\,/\,50.2 on QVHighlights (\vs 48.8\,/\,54.8) and 33.3\,/\,31.0
on ActivityNet at 30\% (\vs 34.7\,/\,32.4). \TODO{full table with the official parser.}
